The ritual

Three steps, once a week.

01

Mix

Combine one teaspoon of SAHRI BEAUTY Nila with rose water until you form a smooth, deep-indigo paste. Let it bloom for a minute.

02

Apply

Sweep onto clean skin with a soft brush, in even strokes. Leave for 10 minutes — until the mask is touch-dry but still cool.

03

Reveal

Rinse with lukewarm water. Pat dry. Skin looks brighter, smoother, more even-toned. Use two to three times a week.
